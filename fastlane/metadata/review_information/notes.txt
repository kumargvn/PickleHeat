Sign in with Apple, or tap "Try the demo" on the first screen to explore with sample data (no account needed).

After signing in, the app asks the player to connect DUPR (the official pickleball rating system) once. In the demo, "Connect my DUPR account" links a sample profile.

Entry fees are for real-world sports events (league seasons and tournaments) and are paid through Stripe to the organizer, so In-App Purchase does not apply (Guideline 3.1.3(e)).

Account deletion: Profile tab > Delete account.
