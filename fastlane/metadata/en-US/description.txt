Pickora is the pickleball app for players who want to know how they're really playing, and organizers who are done with group texts and spreadsheets.

YOUR FORM, NOT JUST YOUR RATING
Connect DUPR once. Pickora reads your last 8 DUPR matches and compares the points you actually won with what your rating predicted. The result is a Form score from 0 to 100: 50 means you're playing right at your rating, 75+ means you're hot.

INSIGHTS FROM YOUR SCORES
• Record, point share and close-game record
• How you do against higher-rated teams
• Player DNA: Clutch, Consistency, Giant-killer and Momentum
• A tip based on your weakest area

LEAGUES AND TOURNAMENTS
• Create a league for your regular group or run a full tournament
• Set a DUPR range; players outside it can't register
• Automatic round robins, snake-seeded pools and standings
• Players enter scores, the other team confirms, and results go to DUPR automatically

ENTRY FEES, PAID TO YOU
Players pay by card or Apple Pay through Stripe. Fees go straight to the organizer's linked bank account.

TOURNAMENTS PICKED FOR YOU
Pickora suggests nearby tournaments with an event that fits your rating, and nudges you up a bracket when you're in hot form.
