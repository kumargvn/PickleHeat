PickleHeat is the pickleball app for players who want to know how they're really playing, and organizers who are done with group texts and spreadsheets.

YOUR FORM, NOT JUST YOUR RATING
Connect DUPR once. PickleHeat reads your last 8 DUPR matches and compares the points you actually won with what your rating predicted. The result is a Form score from 0 to 100: 50 means you're playing right at your rating, 75+ means you're hot.

INSIGHTS FROM YOUR SCORES
• Record, point share and close-game record
• How you do against higher-rated teams
• Player DNA: Clutch, Consistency, Giant-killer and Momentum
• A tip based on your weakest area

HEAT CLIMB LEAGUES
Sign up solo. Each round you're placed in a heat of 4 near your level and play rotating partners. Win your heat to climb; last place drops a heat. Skip a week and you keep your spot, or ask the league for a sub.

WILDFIRE MIXERS
New heat, new partners every round. Standings by total games won.

TEAM LEAGUES AND TOURNAMENTS
• Round robins, pools with playoffs, single and double elimination brackets
• MLP-style team matches with a DreamBreaker, and trips
• Set a DUPR range; players outside it can't register
• Players enter scores, the other side confirms, and results go to DUPR automatically
• Waitlists, invites, and stats like upsets, nailbiters and point differential

CLUBS AND CHAT
• Clubs with owners, admins, member pricing and a club chat
• An event chat for every league and tournament, and a heat chat every round to pick a time and court
• Notifications when it's your turn to play or confirm a score

ENTRY FEES, PAID TO THE ORGANIZER
Organizers collect entry fees on the PickleHeat website, straight to their own Stripe account.

TOURNAMENTS PICKED FOR YOU
PickleHeat suggests nearby tournaments with an event that fits your rating, and nudges you up a bracket when you're in hot form.
