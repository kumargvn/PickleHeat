Formats players actually want

Choose a league style, or run a classic bracket. Everything works on iPhone and on the web.

Signature

Heat Climb

A flex ladder. Sign up solo, play in heats of 4–5 grouped by rank, and rotate partners. The top of each heat climbs, the bottom drops, and skipping a week keeps your spot.

Signature

Wildfire Mixer

A social scramble: a new heat and new partners every round, with individual standings at the end.

Classic

Round robin & pools

Round robins, or pools followed by playoffs, with automatic standings and tiebreakers.

Classic

Brackets

Single or double elimination, seeded the way you choose.

Teams

MLP & trips

Team matches with four lines and a DreamBreaker, plus trips events.

Built in

Chats & notifications

Each heat gets its own chat, plus reminders, subs, attendance, invites and waitlists.

Scores you can trust

Players report results, and organizers have the final say. Nothing is sent to a rating system until the organizer submits it.

Enter the score

A player on either team enters the result right after the match.

Other team confirms

The opposing team confirms the score or disputes it, and standings update once it's confirmed.

Organizer reviews

Confirmed results wait in the league admin's or tournament director's review queue, where they can be edited or deleted.

Organizer submits

The organizer submits the reviewed results for rating, one at a time or a whole round at once. DUPR integration is in progress.

Built for organizers

Clubs, league directors and tournament organizers get one place to run everything.
