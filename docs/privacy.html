Privacy Policy

Pickora · Last updated November 7, 2026

The short version: Pickora uses your information only to run leagues and tournaments, show your DUPR-based insights and process entry fees. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps.

What we collect

  • Account: your name and the email address Apple shares when you use Sign in with Apple (this can be a private relay address).
  • DUPR: when you connect DUPR, your DUPR ID, ratings and recent match results, plus access tokens DUPR issues so Pickora can keep them up to date. You log in on DUPR's own page; Pickora never sees your DUPR password.
  • Events: leagues and tournaments you create or join, teams, schedules and scores.
  • Location: an approximate location (from your phone, if you allow it, or a city you type) to find nearby tournaments.
  • Payments: entry fees are processed by Stripe. Pickora receives the payment status and amount, never your full card number. Organizers who receive fees give their bank and identity details to Stripe, not to Pickora.

How it's used and shared

  • Match results from Pickora events are sent to DUPR so they count toward ratings.
  • Organizers see the names, team names, ratings and payment status of players in their events.
  • Service providers that run Pickora for us (hosting, database, Stripe, DUPR) process data only to provide the service.
  • No advertising, no data brokers, no cross-app tracking.

Your choices

  • Disconnect DUPR any time in Profile. Pickora then deletes the DUPR tokens and match history it stored.
  • Delete your account in Profile → Delete account. This removes your profile, DUPR link and payout link. Results already submitted to DUPR remain on DUPR, and payment records may be kept as the law requires.
  • Location is optional; you can type a city instead.

Children

Pickora is not directed at children under 13.
