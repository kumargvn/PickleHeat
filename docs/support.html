Connecting DUPR

After signing in, tap Connect my DUPR account and log in on DUPR's page. You only do this once. To reconnect, go to Profile → Disconnect DUPR, then connect again.

My score isn't on DUPR yet

A score goes to DUPR after the other team confirms it (or the organizer enters it). Every player in the match needs DUPR connected in PickleHeat.

Entry fees and refunds

Fees are paid to the event's organizer through Stripe. Refunds follow the organizer's policy; organizers can refund an entry from the event page.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete account.
