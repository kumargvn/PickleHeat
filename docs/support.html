Connecting DUPR

After signing in, tap Connect my DUPR account and log in on DUPR's page. You only do this once. To reconnect, go to Profile → Disconnect DUPR, then connect again.

My score isn't on DUPR yet

A score goes to DUPR only after the league admin or tournament organizer reviews it and sends it. Players enter the score and the other team confirms it; that makes it count in PickleHeat and puts it in the organizer's review queue. Organizers can correct or delete a result, keep it off DUPR, or take it back after sending. Every player in the match needs DUPR connected in PickleHeat.

Entry fees and refunds

Fees are paid to the event's organizer through Stripe. Refunds follow the organizer's policy; organizers can refund an entry from the event page.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete account.
